Got Questions?
We’ve Got Answers

Everything you need to know about colouring at home with OneColor. Can’t find your answer? Our team is happy to help.

Know more
What makes OneColor different from regular hair colour?

OneColor features a ready-to-use, no-mix format powered by patented German technology for a simple at-home colouring experience.

Is OneColor suitable for sensitive scalps?

OneColor is ammonia-free and dermatologically tested. As with any permanent colour, do a patch test 48 hours before every application.

How long does the hair colour last?

It’s a permanent colour, so it grows out rather than washing out. Use the extra cream in your pack for 2–3 root touch-ups between full sessions.

How do I choose the right shade?

Use the colour finder above: choose your goal and hair type, then tap a shade to preview it. If you’re between two shades, start with the lighter one.
