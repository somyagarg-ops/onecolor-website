How to use

Four Steps, One Colour.

Wear the gloves from the pack and start on dry, unwashed hair.

  1. 0148 hrs before

    Patch test

    Dab a little cream behind your ear and leave it for 48 hours. Don’t colour if you notice any itching or redness.

  2. 025 min

    Apply from the tube

    Section dry hair and apply the cream straight from the tube, roots first, then lengths.

  3. 0330 min

    Leave to develop

    Leave the colour on for 30 minutes. For root touch-ups, apply to regrowth only.

  4. 045 min

    Rinse and condition

    Rinse until the water runs clear, then use the aftercare conditioner. Save leftover cream for touch-ups.

FAQ

Before You Colour.

Will it cover all my greys?

Yes. The cream is formulated for full grey coverage. On very resistant greys, leave it on for the full 30 minutes and apply to the roots first.

How many packs do I need?

One pack for short to shoulder-length hair, two for hair below the shoulders. Very thick hair may need one extra pack. Leftover cream keeps for 2–3 root touch-ups.

Can I use it on previously coloured or hennaed hair?

You can use it on hair coloured with other permanent dyes, but results may vary. Don’t use it over henna or metallic dyes. Do a strand test first.

How long does the colour last?

It’s permanent, so it grows out rather than washing out. Most people touch up their roots every 4–6 weeks.

Can I mix two shades together?

We don’t recommend mixing shades. Each shade is formulated on its own and hasn’t been tested for blending.

How should I store the leftover cream?

Close the tube tightly and store it in a cool, dry place away from sunlight. Use it within the period shown on the pack.
